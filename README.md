## About Me

I'm a Data Analyst specializing in sales performance, marketing analytics, and business intelligence — turning commercial data into decisions that improve margins, optimize spend, and recover revenue.

Over the past few years I've delivered end-to-end analytics across retail, marketing, and sales operations — building ETL pipelines, star schema models, KPI frameworks, and executive dashboards using SQL, Python, and Power BI. My work is grounded in business outcomes: identifying where revenue leaks, where spend is inefficient, and where the biggest growth levers are.
